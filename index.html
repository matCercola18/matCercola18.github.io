About

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology at Politecnico di Milano, advised by Prof. Simone Formentin, and currently a Visiting Student Researcher at Stanford University's Autonomous Systems Lab with Prof. Marco Pavone.

I study how robot foundation models reason, and whether that reasoning actually controls what they do. My recent work looks at vision–language–action (VLA) models for driving and manipulation: whether their chain-of-thought is faithful to the policy, how to represent reasoning as a distribution over plans that can be sampled and steered, and why a model can perceive a hazard yet fail to act on it. I come to this from control and learning from human feedback, and I care about learning-based systems that are interpretable, steerable, and safe.

Research

Reasoning in VLA models

Does a model's reasoning drive its actions? Faithful chain-of-thought, plans as steerable distributions, and the gap between perceiving a hazard and braking for it.

Faithfulness · Plan latents · Binding gap

Learning from human feedback
